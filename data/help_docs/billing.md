# Billing & Plans

## Plans
Nimbus offers three plans:
- **Free** — up to 3 projects and 2 members.
- **Pro** — unlimited projects, up to 10 members, at 12 EUR per member per month.
- **Business** — unlimited members, advanced permissions, and priority support.

## Upgrading or downgrading
Open **Settings → Billing** and click **Change plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately;
downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

## Payment methods
We accept major credit cards and SEPA direct debit for EU customers. Update your payment
method under **Settings → Billing → Payment method**.

## Invoices
Every invoice is available under **Settings → Billing → Invoices** as a downloadable PDF.
Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing cycle.
