# Teams & Permissions

## Inviting teammates
1. Open **Settings → Members**.
2. Click **Invite member** and enter their email address.
3. Choose a role (see below) and click **Send invite**. They'll receive an email link to
   join your workspace.

## Roles
- **Admin** — full access, including billing and member management.
- **Member** — can create and edit projects and tasks, but cannot manage billing.
- **Viewer** — read-only access to projects they are added to.

## Removing a member
Under **Settings → Members**, click the menu next to a person's name and choose
**Remove**. Their tasks stay in the project and can be reassigned.

## Project-level access
Even within a workspace, you can restrict a project to specific members. Open the project,
go to **Project settings → Access**, and choose who can see it.
