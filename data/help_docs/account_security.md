# Account & Security

## Resetting your password
1. Go to the sign-in page and click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address on your account and click **Send reset link**.
3. Check your inbox for an email from Nimbus and click the link (it expires after 1 hour).
4. Choose a new password. It must be at least 10 characters.

If the reset email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder and make
sure you entered the correct email.

## Enabling two-factor authentication (2FA)
1. Open **Settings → Security**.
2. Click **Enable 2FA** and scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google
   Authenticator, Authy, etc.).
3. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm. Save your backup codes somewhere safe.

## Changing your email address
Open **Settings → Account**, click **Change email**, enter the new address, and confirm
via the verification link we send to the new inbox.
